"pedestal_size"= 6.2	' side of the square pedestal recess under the chip, in mm (pedestal_shape 0). The real chip is 6.25 mm; the square is 0.025 mm smaller on each side, so the die cannot fall in and rests on the rim. Must be above 0 and below 6.25 (see design/pedestal.py).

"pedestal_diameter"= 6.25	' diameter of the circular pedestal recess under the chip, in mm (pedestal_shape 1). The circle may be as big as the chip, 6.25 mm: its edge touches the die's sides at their midpoints. Must be above 0 and at most 6.25 (see design/pedestal.py).

"pedestal_shape"= 0	' shape of the pedestal recess under the chip: 0 = square of pedestal_size with rounded corners (pedestal_corner_radius), 1 = circle of pedestal_diameter. Both are cut into the box; the other one is suppressed.

"pedestal_corner_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the rounded corners of the square pedestal recess (pedestal_shape 0), in mm. The recess corners are milled, and a 1 mm end mill cannot cut a sharper one. Must be above 0 (a SolidWorks fillet cannot be zero) and less than half pedestal_size (see design/pedestal.py). Not used by the circle.

"smp_relief_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the relief circles at the four corners of each SMP connector pocket in the lid, in mm: the 1 mm end mill cuts each corner with a circle of its own radius, centred smp_relief_inset inside the two walls of that corner. The SMP housing is 5.0 x 5.0 mm in plan (4.1 mm high) with its corners cut back about 0.42 mm, so the circles clear it. Must be above 0, and the circle must cover the window's sharp corner (smp_relief_inset x sqrt 2 below it). Built by base_parts/add_smp_relief.py.

"smp_relief_inset"= 0.6 ' how far in, in mm, each relief circle is centred inside the two walls of its pocket corner (the circle bites smp_relief_radius - smp_relief_inset into the lid). 0 puts the centre on the corner (the old dog-bone, 3 pi/4 R^2 removed per corner); 0.6 leaves 0.4 mm of bite. Changing it needs add_smp_relief.py --redo (the centres are placed by coordinate). Must be at least 0 and below smp_relief_radius / sqrt 2. The layout check (design/constraints.py) refuses insets that leave too little lid.

"chip_pocket_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the four inside corners of the 7.5 mm chip window in the lid plug, in mm. The window is milled, and a 1 mm end mill cannot cut a sharper inside corner. Must be above 0 (a SolidWorks fillet cannot be zero). Built by base_parts/add_chip_pocket_radius.py.

"cavity_corner_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the four vertical inside corners of the box cavity, in mm, and of the matching corners of the lid plug that goes into it. The cavity is milled, and a 1 mm end mill cannot cut a sharper inside corner. Must be above 0 (a SolidWorks fillet cannot be zero) and less than half the cavity's smaller side (see design/constraints.py).

"pcb_corner_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the four vertical corners of the PCB, in mm. The board sits in the box cavity with no gap, so it must be at least the cavity radius (cavity_corner_radius) to fit inside the cavity's rounded corners; it may be larger. Must be above 0 and less than half the board's smaller side (see design/constraints.py).

"bonding_clearence"= 0.6	' how far out from the die the lid must stay clear; the bond fingers sit midway between the PCB opening and this

"no_distortion" = 1 ' (boolean) when set to 1, no distortions are present. when set to 0, distortions are added to the box and holes to the PCB.

"no_chip" = 0 ' (boolean) when set to 1, the bare board: no chip, opening, pedestal or wire bonds, just the CPW from one connector to the other. 0 builds the full package.

"screws_on" = 1 ' (boolean) when set to 1, four M2 countersunk screws hold the lid, PCB and box together at the PCB corners. 0 builds without them.

"screw_inset" = 3.5 ' distance from the PCB edge to the screw centre, on both axes (the 4.28 mm countersink must stay inside the lid outline)

"screw_clearance_diameter" = 2.3 ' hole in the lid and the PCB (M2 is 2.0 mm, plus the margin a drill and a router need)

"screw_tap_diameter" = 1.6 ' tap-drill hole in the box floor the screw cuts its thread into

"screw_keepout_diameter" = 3.0 ' no copper or via inside this circle round each PCB hole

"screw_engagement" = 3 ' length of thread in the box: the tapped hole goes right through the floor, so this is the floor thickness at the PCB corners (3 mm is 1.5 x the screw diameter, the usual minimum for steel in aluminium)

"floor_width" = IIF ( "screws_on" > 0.5 , IIF ( "screw_engagement" - "pedestal_depth" > "wall_width" , "screw_engagement" - "pedestal_depth" , "wall_width" ) , "wall_width" ) ' box floor under the chip pocket (the floor under the rest of the board is floor_width + pedestal_depth thick); worked out, not typed: with the screws on, thick enough that the through-tapped hole has screw_engagement of thread, and never thinner than wall_width

"lid_width" = 1.5 ' thickness of lid_inside (was 1.0 mm); must be at least the 1.14 mm head plus 0.3 mm of seat under the countersink, 1.44 mm

"screw_length" = 0 ' length of the screw as cut, head top to tip; 0 means work it out from the stack (lid + board + floor, so the tip is flush with the underside of the box)

"vents_on" = 1 ' (boolean) when set to 1, narrow vent grooves in the top of the box floor let the air trapped under the die (the pedestal recess) and in the screw bores out of the side of the box. 0 builds without them.

"vent_width" = 0.5 ' width of each vent groove (a 0.5 mm groove has a 300 GHz cutoff, over ten times the 26 GHz band top)

"vent_depth" = 0.3 ' depth of each vent groove, down from the top face of the box floor (the face the PCB rests on)
