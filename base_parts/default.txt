"pedestal_diameter"= 6.25	' diameter of the circular pedestal recess under the chip, in mm (pedestal_shape 1). The circle may be as big as the chip, 6.25 mm: its edge touches the die's sides at their midpoints. Must be above 0 and at most 6.25 (see design/pedestal.py).

"pedestal_shape"= 0	' shape of the pedestal recess under the chip: 0 = square of pedestal_size with rounded corners (pedestal_corner_radius), 1 = circle of pedestal_diameter. Both are cut into the box; the other one is suppressed.

"pedestal_corner_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the rounded corners of the square pedestal recess (pedestal_shape 0), in mm. The recess corners are milled, and a 1 mm end mill cannot cut a sharper one. "smp_window_extra"= 0.25	' how far each SMP window in the lid grows on each of its four sides, in mm, about its centre (0.25: 5.1 -> 5.6 mm). The 5.0 mm housing must fit: design/constraints.py check_smp_window refuses less than 0.15 mm. Built by base_parts/add_smp_window.py.

"smp_window_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the four rounded corners of each SMP connector window in the lid, in mm: the 1 mm end mill cannot cut a sharp inside corner, so the window is milled with R1 arcs. "chip_pocket_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the four inside corners of the 7.5 mm chip window in the lid plug, in mm. The window is milled, and a 1 mm end mill cannot cut a sharper inside corner. Must be above 0 (a SolidWorks fillet cannot be zero). Built by base_parts/add_chip_pocket_radius.py.

"cavity_corner_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the four vertical inside corners of the box cavity, in mm, and of the matching corners of the lid plug that goes into it. The cavity is milled, and a 1 mm end mill cannot cut a sharper inside corner. Must be above 0 (a SolidWorks fillet cannot be zero) and less than half the cavity's smaller side (see design/constraints.py).

"pcb_corner_radius"= 1.0	' radius of the four vertical corners of the PCB, in mm. The board sits in the box cavity with no gap, so it must be at least the cavity radius (cavity_corner_radius) to fit inside the cavity's rounded corners; it may be larger. "vent_depth" = 0.3 ' depth of each vent groove, down from the top face of the box floor (the face the PCB rests on)
